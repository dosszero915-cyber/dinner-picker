Chana masala

🍲
IndiaTraditional
35 minto cook
380kcal per serving
15 gprotein
Vegetarian

Where it comes from

A chickpea curry from the Punjab region of North India, eaten for generations as everyday home food.

Ingredients

  • 🌰 Chickpeas
  • 🍅 Tomatoes
  • 🧅 Onion
  • 🧄 Garlic

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Fry onion and garlic with cumin and coriander.
  2. Add chopped tomato and cook down for 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in chickpeas and simmer for 15 minutes.
