Cottage pie

🥧
United Kingdom1791
50 minto cook
520kcal per serving
30 gprotein

Where it comes from

The name “cottage pie” was first recorded in 1791, when potatoes were a food of the poor.

Ingredients

  • 🥩 Ground beef
  • 🥔 Potatoes
  • 🥕 Carrots
  • 🧅 Onion

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Brown beef with onion and carrot.
  2. Boil and mash the potatoes with butter.
  3. Top the meat with the mash and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20 minutes.
