Where it comes from

The glazed teriyaki cooking method dates to Japan’s Edo period (1603 to 1868).

Ingredients

  • 🐟 Salmon
  • 🍚 Rice
  • 🥦 Broccoli
  • 🧄 Garlic

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Cook the rice and steam the broccoli.
  2. Pan-fry the salmon for 4 minutes per side.
  3. Glaze with soy sauce, garlic and a pinch of sugar, then serve over rice.
