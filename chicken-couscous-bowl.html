Chicken couscous bowl

🥣
Morocco, Algeria, TunisiaAbout 1,000 years old
30 minto cook
560kcal per serving
44 gprotein

Where it comes from

UNESCO recognized couscous as shared cultural heritage of the Maghreb in 2020.

Ingredients

  • 🍗 Chicken
  • 🥣 Couscous
  • 🥒 Zucchini
  • 🌰 Chickpeas

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Cover couscous with hot water for 5 minutes.
  2. Pan-fry diced chicken with cumin and paprika.
  3. Add zucchini and chickpeas for 6 minutes.
  4. Fluff the couscous and top with the chicken.
