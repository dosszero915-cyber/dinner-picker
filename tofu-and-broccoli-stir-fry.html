Tofu and broccoli stir-fry

🥦
ChinaAbout 2,000 years ago
25 minto cook
430kcal per serving
24 gprotein
Vegetarian

Where it comes from

Tofu is credited to China during the Han dynasty, roughly two thousand years ago.

Ingredients

  • 🧈 Tofu
  • 🥦 Broccoli
  • 🫑 Bell pepper
  • 🍚 Rice

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Press and cube the tofu, then fry until crisp.
  2. Add broccoli and sliced pepper with soy sauce and a splash of water.
  3. Cover for 3 minutes and serve over rice.
