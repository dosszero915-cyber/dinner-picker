How the dinner picker works

Choose everything you have, from chicken and eggs to onions and rice. The picker compares your plate with its recipes and shows dishes you can make with at least half of the ingredients, closest match first.

“Ready to cook” means you need nothing else. Otherwise you’ll see exactly what’s missing. We assume you always have oil, salt, pepper, common spices, soy sauce and butter.

About the numbers and the stories

Calories and protein are rough estimates for one serving and will change with your portions and brands. Dish histories are simplified, and many classic dishes have disputed origins.

This tool offers general information, not medical or dietary advice. If you have a health condition or food allergy, ask a doctor or registered dietitian.
