Gambas al ajillo

🦐
SpainTraditional
15 minto cook
330kcal per serving
28 gprotein

Where it comes from

A sizzling garlic shrimp tapa found in bars across Spain.

Ingredients

  • 🦐 Shrimp
  • 🧄 Garlic
  • 🍋 Lemon
  • 🍞 Bread or wraps

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Gently fry sliced garlic in oil with chili if you like.
  2. Add shrimp and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. Finish with lemon and mop up the oil with bread.
