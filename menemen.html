Where it comes from

A soft scramble of eggs, tomato and peppers named after the town of Menemen near İzmir.

Ingredients

  • 🥚 Eggs
  • 🍅 Tomatoes
  • 🫑 Bell pepper
  • 🧅 Onion

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Cook onion and pepper in oil until soft.
  2. Add chopped tomato and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the eggs gently until just set.
