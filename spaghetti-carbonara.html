Spaghetti carbonara

🍝
ItalyMid-1900s
25 minto cook
620kcal per serving
28 gprotein

Where it comes from

Most food historians place its birth in Rome in the 1940s. The classic recipe uses eggs, hard cheese and cured pork, and no cream.

Ingredients

  • 🍝 Pasta
  • 🥚 Eggs
  • 🧀 Cheese
  • 🥓 Bacon

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Boil the pasta.
  2. Crisp the bacon in a pan.
  3. Whisk eggs with grated cheese and black pepper.
  4. Off the heat, toss everything with a splash of pasta water until creamy.
