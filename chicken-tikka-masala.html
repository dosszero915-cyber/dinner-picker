Chicken tikka masala

🍛
India and the UK1970s (disputed)
40 minto cook
560kcal per serving
40 gprotein

Where it comes from

Britain and India both claim it. Many stories place it in a Glasgow or Birmingham restaurant in the 1970s, built on grilled Indian chicken tikka.

Ingredients

  • 🍗 Chicken
  • 🥛 Yogurt
  • 🍅 Tomatoes
  • 🧅 Onion
  • 🧄 Garlic
  • 🍚 Rice

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt, garlic and spices.
  2. Fry onion, add chopped tomato and simmer to a sauce.
  3. Add the chicken and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Serve over rice.
