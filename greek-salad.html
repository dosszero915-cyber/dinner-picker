Vegetarian

Where it comes from

The tomato and cucumber version served in tavernas became standard in the 20th century.

Ingredients

  • 🍅 Tomatoes
  • 🥒 Zucchini
  • 🧅 Onion
  • 🫒 Olives
  • 🧀 Cheese

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Chop tomato and zucchini or cucumber.
  2. Add thin onion, olives and cubed cheese.
  3. Dress with olive oil, lemon and salt.
