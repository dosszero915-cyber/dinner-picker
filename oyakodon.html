Where it comes from

This chicken and egg rice bowl is commonly credited to a Tokyo restaurant in 1891. Its name means “parent and child”.

Ingredients

  • 🍗 Chicken
  • 🥚 Eggs
  • 🧅 Onion
  • 🍚 Rice

Plus oil, salt, pepper, spices and soy sauce if you have them.

Method

  1. Simmer sliced onion and chicken in soy sauce and a little water.
  2. Pour in beaten eggs and cover for 1 minute.
  3. Slide over hot rice.
